### Why use paragraphs instead of line numbers?
> [ You have to keep in mind that line numbers are generated at display
time, making them ephemeral and essentially meaningless. Change margins
or font size or paper size or paper orientation and all the line numbers
will change (with greater change the further into the document you go.) 
I can load the same document on Windows and macOS and all the line
numbers will be different after the first few pages.  Because of this,
there's no reference to line numbers in the document file itself,
meaning there's no reference for you to grab onto for what you want.  In
my experience, paragraph numbers will be more reliable for what you want
than line numbers. ](https://github.com/python-openxml/python-docx/issues/1073#issuecomment-1096809115)
